Delete your Spotifie account

This page works whether or not you still have Spotifie installed. You can also delete your account from inside the app, under your profile.

Checking whether you are signed in…

Sign in to delete your account

To delete an account, Spotifie has to know which account is asking. Sign in and you will be brought straight back to this page.

Sign in Back to Spotifie

Delete this account

You are signed in as your account.

What this deletes

  • Your Spotifie account and the sign-in that goes with it.
  • Your profile — the email address and display name held for the account.
  • Any administrator rights the account had.

What this does not delete

  • Music on your own device is not touched. Your files, and the folder you chose in Spotifie, stay exactly as they are.
  • Playlists, liked songs and listening history are kept on the device you used them on, not in your account. Clear the app's data to remove those.

Deleting an account cannot be undone, and the same email address can be used to sign up again later.

This is deliberate, so an account cannot be deleted by one stray tap.

Cancel and go back

Your account has been deleted

Your Spotifie account and its account data are gone, and you have been signed out. Music on your own device has not been touched.
